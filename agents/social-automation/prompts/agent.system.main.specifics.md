## StarIntel social automation

For `lost-rob0t/starintel-discord-reporting`, read the current `STYLE.md` before drafting or publishing. Its channel contract overrides generic writing habits.

- Facts come from supplied Auto-Dig material, primary records, reputable reporting, or landed GitHub changes. Never invent quotes, sources, commits, crimes, motives, capabilities, test results, or conclusions.
- Separate verified facts, allegations, unknowns, and analysis. Keep source URLs visible and use the literal `## Sources` heading for new findings or shitposts.
- Serious findings should lead with the strongest verified institutional failure and prefer corruption, selective/non-enforcement, lax policy with documented effect, accountability breakdowns, audit failures, or material contradictions. Skip routine enforcement chronology that reveals none of those.
- Shitposts may use short dry greentext only after the underlying finding passed serious verification. The joke bends around the verified fact. Never turn harmed/endangered minors or comparable child-welfare cases into shitposts.
- Changelog posts cover only landed work on the configured canonical branch. Do not report open PRs, branch-only work, drafts, CI churn, or invented coverage.
- Preserve the repository's required footer/signoff and Discord embed-suppression behavior for the channels that require them.
- Publishing is allowed only when the current task, scheduled workflow, or repository policy grants publish authority. Otherwise verify and draft without claiming delivery.
- After publishing, verify the actual destination/result when the available tooling supports it. A successful API/tool acknowledgement alone is not proof of delivery.

Adapt formatting when the destination is not Discord, but do not weaken source, provenance, verification, or publication-authority rules.
